Laundry Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page runs pipeline arithmetic over the cycle times you enter: when the last load is dry, which machine is the bottleneck, the latest start that still makes your done-by time, and when each load lands. It does not know your machines' real cycle times, whether a load is dry-clean only, or when you should do laundry, and it gives no verdict on your machines or your clothes. Every number here is your own entry.

The loads

How many loads, and the minutes each machine takes per load: whole numbers from 1 to 600.

The finish truth

Finish: the last load out of the dryer, measured from the first load's start.

The bottleneck truth

Bottleneck: which machine the pipeline waits on, and how long the other sits idle.

The deadline truth

Deadline: the latest start that still makes your done-by time.

The handoff truth

Handoff: when each load lands dry, one by one.
